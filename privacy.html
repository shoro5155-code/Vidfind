Privacy

VidFind lets you search YouTube and selected public video collections. It uses YouTube API Services for YouTube search results and the official YouTube embedded player for playback.

Your search terms and requested video identifiers are sent to the selected provider: Google/YouTube, NASA or Internet Archive. The server temporarily caches public search results and video metadata for up to five minutes. VidFind does not require an account, request access to your Google account or store a personal search history.

Your browser contacts the video and image providers to load their content. Those providers can receive your IP address, browser information and page origin, and can use their own cookies or similar technologies. The privacy-enhanced YouTube player is the default; the standard player is available as an alternative.

VidFind does not add advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Its hosting provider may retain normal technical request logs. No YouTube video files are copied or stored by VidFind.

Read the Google Privacy Policy, YouTube Terms of Service, NASA Privacy Policy and Internet Archive policies. You can stop using VidFind at any time and clear provider cookies through your browser settings.
